# ch02-ex2E-q1 — working

Solve, giving your answers correct to 3 significant figures.

**a** $5^{x}=18$

Take logs of both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log 5^{x} & =\log 18 \\
x \log 5 & =\log 18 \\
x & =\frac{\log 18}{\log 5}
\end{aligned}
$$

So $x=\frac{\log 18}{\log 5} \approx 1.80$ to 3 significant figures.

**b** $2^{x}=35$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log 2^{x} & =\log 35 \\
x \log 2 & =\log 35 \\
x & =\frac{\log 35}{\log 2} \approx 5.13
\end{aligned}
$$

---

Check: $2^{5.13}=35.0 \checkmark$

Note: $\log$ here means $\log _{10}$; any consistent base gives the same ratio.
